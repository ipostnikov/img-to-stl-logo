Logo → STL

Turn a PNG/JPG/SVG logo into a correctly-oriented, extruded STL — in as many sizes as you like.

1. Image

Drag on the image to erase. Erased areas are filled with the detected background colour (or made transparent if the image already has an alpha channel), so they drop out of the silhouette.

2. Silhouette detection

Only used for images without transparency. Higher = more pixels are treated as background. Watch the "Detected shape" preview and adjust until it matches the logo.

3. Orientation & mesh

Mirroring is what you want when the logo will be pressed/embossed into another surface. "Lay flat" turns the model so its thickness runs vertically off the print bed.

auto

Left-most = no reduction (full detail). Move right to decimate the mesh toward a smaller triangle count — smaller files, coarser edges. Also try "Edge smoothing" above, which reduces triangles by simplifying the outline itself.
